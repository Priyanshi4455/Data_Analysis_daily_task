# 🍔 Online Food Delivery Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Online Food Delivery Analysis Dashboard** created to analyze customer demographics, customer types, feedback, food delivery preferences, occupation, age groups, and monthly income.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of customer behavior and food delivery insights using an Excel-based data analysis approach.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze an online food delivery dataset and identify useful patterns in:

- Customer demographics
- Customer gender distribution
- Customer age groups
- Customer types
- Customer feedback
- Food delivery preferences
- Occupation-wise delivery preferences
- Monthly income distribution

The analysis is presented through an interactive dashboard for easier interpretation of the data.

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

The dashboard contains multiple visualizations to understand customer behavior:

### 👥 Customer Distribution by Gender
Shows the percentage distribution of customers based on gender.

### 💬 Customer Feedback by Customer Type
Compares positive and negative feedback across:

- Frequent Customers
- New Customers
- Regular Customers

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Customer Distribution by Type
Displays the proportion of:

- Frequent Customers
- New Customers
- Regular Customers

### 🎂 Customer Distribution by Age Group
Analyzes customers across different age groups:

- 18–22
- 23–27
- 28–33

### 🚴 Food Delivery Output by Occupation
Compares food delivery responses across different occupations such as:

- Employee
- Housewife
- Self Employed
- Student

### 💰 Customer Distribution by Monthly Income
Analyzes customers based on their monthly income categories.

### 🎛️ Customer Type Filter
An interactive filter is included to analyze the dashboard based on customer type.
